How it works

From first call to final design.

Whether you're a seasoned do-it-yourselfer or hiring a professional crew, selecting products is easy with TrueSource. You'll make thoughtful choices and understand why each one works for your life.

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    Review the scope of your project, your goals and budget, and schedule a jobsite appointment.

  2. 02

    Preliminary quote & CAD drawings

    A quote with manufacturer, door style, wood species and colour, plus drawings to review together.

  3. 03

    On-site consultation

    We inspect the jobsite, confirm every measurement and refine the design to match real conditions.

  4. 04

    Final design & order

    Your updated quote becomes a contract, materials are ordered and you receive lead-time details.
